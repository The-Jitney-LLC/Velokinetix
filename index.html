Services

Your systems, connected and monitored

Workflow builds

We map how work moves through your business today, then build automations that carry each inquiry, order or record from one system to the next without retyping.

Systems integration

Our packages connect 2 to 8 of your systems so they share the same data: website forms, CRMs, order tools, Google Sheets, email and more. Anything beyond 8 is a custom workflow build.

About · Capability statement

Velo Kinetix Systems

Seamless System Solutions

Who we are

Velo Kinetix Systems designs, builds and monitors business workflow automation. We connect the software a company already runs so that inquiries, orders and records move between systems on their own, and we watch those connections every day so failures are caught and fixed fast. Our packages are priced for small and mid-sized businesses, with a fixed setup fee and a predictable monthly plan.

Core competencies

  • Workflow automation design and build
  • Systems integration: 2 to 8 platforms per package, unlimited with a custom build
  • Website form and inquiry pipelines
  • Order workflow automation
  • CRM, spreadsheet and email data sync
  • Workflow monitoring with failure alerts
  • Ongoing changes on monthly hours
  • Custom workflow builds with no module limit

Differentiators

  • Fixed prices: one setup fee and one monthly fee per package, published openly
  • Scope you can count: workflows, systems and change hours stated for every package
  • Process first: we fix a broken process before we automate it, so you never get efficient mistakes
  • Failure alerts included in every package
  • The same fast, hands-on support for every client, whatever the package
  • Priced below senior fractional consultants for comparable monthly support

Package range

  • 1 to 10 automated workflows
  • 2 to 8 connected systems, more with a custom build
  • 2 to 20 change hours per month
  • Setup from $3,500 to $18,000
  • Monthly plans from $650 to $3,800

Who we serve

Small and mid-sized businesses whose teams spend hours moving data by hand between forms, order systems, CRMs and spreadsheets.
